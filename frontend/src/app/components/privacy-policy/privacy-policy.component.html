Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) & HL7 FHIR Interoperability Statement

Effective Date: August 2, 2026 Version: 1.0.0-ENTERPRISE-GOLD Governance: 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164 (HIPAA)
Legal & Regulatory Commitment Statement

MedVault Systems ("MedVault", "we", "our", or "us") operates an enterprise Electronic Health Record (EHR) platform designed to store, manage, and transmit Protected Health Information (PHI) under strict adherence to federal healthcare privacy laws. This Privacy Policy governs our compliance with the HIPAA Privacy and Security Rules (45 CFR § 164.308 / § 164.312), the 21st Century Cures Act, and the HL7 FHIR R4 interoperability standard.

1. Scope & System Governance

This policy applies to all clinical users (Physicians, Nurses, Hospital Administrators, Compliance Auditors) and Patients accessing the MedVault platform via web browsers, API endpoints (`/api/v1/...`), or HL7 FHIR R4 REST services (`/fhir/v1/...`). By creating an account or accessing patient records within MedVault, you acknowledge the terms of this Privacy Policy and agree to abide by all applicable clinical role permissions.

2. Categories of Information Collected

Protected Health Information (PHI)

  • Master Patient Index (MPI): Full Name, DOB, Gender, SSN, MRN code.
  • Clinical Encounter Logs: SOAP Progress notes, chief complaints, visit types.
  • Bedside Vitals: Blood pressure, heart rate, SpO2, BMI, glucose.
  • Coded Diagnoses: Problem lists mapped to ICD-10 & SNOMED CT.
  • eRx Pharmacy Orders: Prescriptions, dosages, routes, RxNorm codes.
  • Allergy Register: Allergen substances, severity ratings, ADR histories.

System Audit & Technical Metadata

  • User Credentials: Hashed passwords, assigned RBAC roles.
  • JWT Authentication Tokens: Claims, issuing timestamps, expirations.
  • WORM Audit Trails: User ID, IP address, request URI, target entity ID.
  • HTTP Request Headers: User-Agent, Accept header (`application/fhir+json`).
  • Session Context: Active patient chart selections by clinicians.

3. HIPAA Technical Safeguards (§ 164.312)

Stateless JWT Bearer Token Security

Authentication utilizes JSON Web Tokens (JWT) signed with HMAC SHA-512 cryptographic keys. Tokens expire automatically after 24 hours to prevent unauthorized access from idle sessions.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Method-Level Security

Every API endpoint is protected by Spring Security `@PreAuthorize` rules. Clinicians (`ROLE_DOCTOR`, `ROLE_NURSE`) can view patient records, while administrative functions are strictly reserved for `ROLE_ADMIN` and compliance functions for `ROLE_AUDITOR`.

Smart Safety Engine & Clinical Quality Controls

Prescription requests automatically trigger computerized drug-allergy contraindication checks against the patient's active allergy register prior to order confirmation.

4. Immutable WORM Audit Ledger (HIPAA § 164.312(b))

MedVault maintains a Write-Once-Read-Many (WORM) audit trail ledger in the database (`audit_logs`). Any action involving patient data creation, view, update, export, or deletion generates an immutable audit record containing:

[AUDIT LOG ENTRY] Timestamp | User ID | Role | IP Address | Action (READ/WRITE/EXPORT) | Target Patient ID

5. HL7 FHIR R4 Interoperability & 21st Century Cures Act

In accordance with ONC Interoperability rules and the 21st Century Cures Act, MedVault guarantees patient data ownership and open data portability:

  • Patient `$everything` Export: Patients and authorized clinicians can export complete longitudinal clinical records in open `application/fhir+json` format.
  • Standard Codings: Data is serialized using international healthcare standards including LOINC (vitals), SNOMED CT & ICD-10 (problem lists), and RxNorm (eRx orders).
  • CapabilityStatement (`GET /fhir/v1/metadata`): Publicly advertises server capabilities and supported resource types without disclosing PHI.

6. Your Rights Under HIPAA Privacy Rule

Right to Inspect & Copy Request access or 1-click FHIR JSON downloads of your complete medical record.
Right to Request Amendment Request corrections to inaccurate clinical notes or demographic information.
Right to Accounting Obtain a detailed log of all disclosures of your PHI for treatment or compliance audits.
